### Data Warehouse Analysis

Used **SQL Data Warehouse** to analyze customer, product, order, and sales data and generate country-wise business insights.

* **Country Analysis:** Performed country-wise analysis using **fact_sales, dim_customers, and dim_products** to identify the **total number of customers, total orders, total products, and total sales**.
* **Sales Analysis:** Used **SQL queries and aggregations** to analyze sales performance across different countries.
* Used a **Star Schema** with **fact_sales, dim_customers, and dim_products** to support analytical reporting.
