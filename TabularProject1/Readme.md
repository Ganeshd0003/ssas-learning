### SalesDB Analysis

Used **SalesDB** to analyze employee, customer, order, and product data and generate business reports.

* **Employee Analysis:** Generated a gender-wise employee report in Excel, including **average, minimum, maximum, and total salary**.
* **Customer Analysis:** Performed country-wise customer analysis to identify the **number of customers, total sales, average product price, minimum and maximum prices, and customer scores**.
* Used **SQL and SSAS/DAX measures** to perform aggregations and derive business insights from the sales data.
